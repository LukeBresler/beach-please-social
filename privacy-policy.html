Privacy Policy

Beach, please! — and the Beach, Please App Instagram integration

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy covers the Beach, Please App Meta Developer App (used for Instagram integration) and the "Beach, please!" iOS app (Cape Town Beach & Tidal Pool Finder) it belongs to, both operated by the same developer, Luke Bresler ("we," "us"). It explains what data the app collects, what it doesn't, and how the Meta/Instagram integration works.

In short:
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Location is coarse, on-device only, and never leaves your phone.
  • Our Instagram integration only posts to our own account — it doesn't touch other people's Instagram or Facebook data.

1. Information We Collect

Location

With your permission, the app requests a single, coarse (city-block-level, not precise-GPS-level) location fix, only while the app is in use — never in the background. This is used to sort beaches and tidal pools by distance from you.

That location is cached only on your device, so the home-screen widget can reuse it without requesting its own GPS fix. It is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services; the app simply falls back to a score-only ranking.

App usage signals (for the App Store review prompt)

The app records, locally on your device only and never transmitted anywhere, the date you first opened it and the date of your first positive interaction (such as opening a beach in Maps). This is used solely to time Apple's native App Store review prompt.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise or background location, advertising identifiers, or any other personal data. There is no account system, so we have no database of users.

2. Live Conditions Data (Supabase)

The app shows live wind, tide, and water-temperature conditions for Cape Town's beaches, fetched read-only from a shared database using a public, read-only key. This request sends no personal or device-identifying data — it's identical for every user and simply returns the latest shared conditions. The underlying weather, tide, and sea-temperature figures come from third-party providers, refreshed on a fixed schedule by our own backend, never triggered by or containing data about an individual user.

3. The Meta Developer App ("Beach, Please App") and Instagram

Separately from the consumer-facing iOS app, we operate a Meta Developer App named Beach, Please App. Its only purpose is to let our own automated backend publish scheduled Instagram posts (beach highlights and Cape Town facts) to our own Instagram Business account, using the Instagram Graph API.

  • This is a first-party, server-to-server integration — it posts on our own behalf, to our own account.
  • It does not implement Facebook or Instagram Login for other people, and no member of the public authenticates through it.
  • It does not collect, access, or store data belonging to any other Instagram or Facebook user, page, or account.
  • It is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Access credentials are held as encrypted secrets in our automation pipeline, not accessible to app users.

4. Third Parties and SDKs

The iOS app has exactly one third-party dependency — the official Supabase SDK, used only as described above. We do not include any advertising network, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK.

5. Data Retention

Because we don't collect personal data or operate user accounts, there is nothing to retain, export, or delete on our end. The only stored values — your cached coarse location and local review-prompt timestamps — live solely on your device and are removed automatically if you delete the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience recreational tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, since it does not collect personal information at all.

7. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and reflect material changes in the app's release notes.
